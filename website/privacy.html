Privacy Policy

BOOMFORGE (Android package com.boomforge.game). Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Summary

  • When you first open the game, you choose: play as a guest, or continue with Google.
  • As a guest there is no account and your progress stays on your phone. With Google, your email is stored and your progress is backed up to Google Firebase.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports can be turned off in Settings → Share Usage Data.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data in the game or on this website.
  • We do not sell your data.

Who we are

BOOMFORGE is developed and published by the BOOMFORGE team ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, use the form on the account page. You can also email navaparameetdev@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

The game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars, scores, coins, upgrades, unlocked skins and themes, achievements, daily reward and challenge dates) and your settings on your device. Uninstalling the game or using Settings → Reset Progress deletes this copy.

Cloud save and accounts

As a guest, no account is created and your progress is not uploaded. It stays on your phone, so uninstalling the game deletes it.

If you choose Continue with Google, when you first open the game or later in Settings, your Google account's email address, name and account ID are stored using Google Firebase Authentication. A copy of your progress (the same items listed above, plus the app version and platform) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, so you can restore it after reinstalling or on another phone.

Only you can read your cloud save. We use it only to provide cloud saving. Sign Out in Settings stops uploading and keeps your cloud save for your next sign-in.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports

With Share Usage Data switched on (the default), the game uses:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase to record how the game is used: screens opened, buttons tapped, your guest or Google choice, levels started and finished, bosses defeated, elements and power-ups used, ads watched, and upgrades and items bought. These go with device and app information (model, OS version, app version, approximate country) and an app-instance ID, not your name or email.
  • Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports with technical information about your device and the state of the app when it crashed.

We use this to balance levels and fix bugs. Switch it off at any time in Settings → Share Usage Data.

Advertising

The game may show optional ads from Google AdMob (rewarded ads you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between games). Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address and ad interaction data to show, measure and, where you consent, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Who processes your data

Google LLC provides Firebase and AdMob and processes the data above for us. See the Firebase privacy and security information. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Security

All data is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Security rules make sure each player can access only their own cloud save.

How long we keep data

  • Your cloud save is kept until you delete it.
  • Analytics events are kept for up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports are kept for 90 days.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Cloud Save → Delete Cloud Data immediately deletes your cloud save and your account.
  • On the web: go to the Delete account page. If you signed in with Google, you can delete your account there yourself, or send a request with the Player ID shown in Settings → Cloud Save. We complete requests within 30 days. Guests have no account or cloud data to delete.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the copy on your phone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • access, correct, delete or export your data;
  • object to or limit how it is used;
  • complain to your data protection authority.

Use the request form to use these rights.

Permissions

The game only uses internet access, for cloud save, statistics and ads. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

BOOMFORGE is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, use the request form and we will delete it.

Changes

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
