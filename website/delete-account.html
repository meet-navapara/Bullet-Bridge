Delete your BOOMFORGE account

This deletes your BOOMFORGE account and its cloud save: levels, stars, scores, coins, upgrades, skins, settings and the linked email address. It cannot be undone.

Option 1: in the game (fastest)

Open Settings → Cloud Save → Delete Cloud Data. Your account and cloud save are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Signed in with Google? Delete it here

Sign in with the Google account you used in the game, then confirm.

Signed in as .

Option 3: Playing as a guest? Send a request

Guest accounts can't sign in on the web. Copy your Player ID from Settings → Cloud Save in the game and send it below. We delete the account and all of its data within 30 days. Uninstalled already? Send the request without an ID and describe your account; we'll do our best to find it.

You can also use this form for other privacy questions or requests.

DataWhat happens
