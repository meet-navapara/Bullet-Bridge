## Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, unlocked cosmetics, best
score, achievements, daily reward and challenge dates) and your settings
locally on your device. We do not operate servers and do not receive this
information. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset Progress" deletes it.

## Cloud save and accounts (Google Firebase)

To back up your progress, the game creates an anonymous account with
Google Firebase Authentication and stores a copy of your game progress in
Google Cloud Firestore. The copy contains levels, stars, scores, coins,
upgrades, unlocked items, game settings and timestamps. The game does not
ask for your name or email.

If you choose "Sign in with Google", your Google account's email address
and account ID are linked to your save, so you can restore your progress
after reinstalling or on another phone.

You can delete your cloud save and account at any time in the game:
Settings → Cloud Save → Delete Cloud Data. You can also ask us to delete it
at [CONTACT EMAIL]. Progress stored only on your phone is removed when you
uninstall the game.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

The game has no chat or public profiles.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

[KEEP THIS SECTION ONLY IF ADS ARE ENABLED IN YOUR RELEASE.]

The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional full-screen ads
between games, provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process:

- device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID / iOS IDFA (iOS
  only if you allow tracking),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- information about ad interactions and app/device diagnostics.

This is used to serve, measure and (where you consent) personalise ads,
and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions
where required, we ask for your consent first; you can change your choice
at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Analytics

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous gameplay
events: levels started and finished, bosses defeated, upgrades bought.
It uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports, which contain
technical device information and the state of the app at the crash. This
helps us balance levels and fix bugs. You can turn both off in Settings →
Share Usage Data.

## Permissions

Internet access is used only to load ads. The game does not access your
location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
